# OpenTally Desk Research Reference Catalog

## 1. Official Vendor Reference Sources
1. **Tally Solutions Help & Reference Library**:
   - TallyPrime User Guide (Releases 1.0 through 4.1): Company creation, accounting vouchers, inventory, GST compliance, banking, payroll.
   - TallyPrime Edit Log Guide: Requirements for MCA compliance (Companies Audit and Auditors Rules, 2014 Amendment).
2. **Tally Developer & Interoperability Documentation**:
   - Tally XML Data Interchange Reference (HTTP Port 9000 interface, XML request and response envelopes, TallyMessage schema).
   - TDL (Tally Definition Language) Developer Reference: Master and Voucher schema hierarchies, Collection definitions, and Action tags (`IMPORT`, `EXPORT`).
   - Tally ODBC Driver & Schema Guide: Standard table schemas, relational views of Groups, Ledgers, Stock Items, and Vouchers.

## 2. Statutory & Regulatory References
1. **Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Ministry of Finance, Govt of India**:
   - Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (CGST Act) & Rules.
   - Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (IGST Act).
   - Section 170: Rounding off of tax to the nearest rupee.
   - GST Return Formats: GSTR-1 (Outward supplies), GSTR-3B (Summary return), GSTR-2B (Auto-drafted ITC statement).
2. **Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Govt of India**:
   - Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014 — Rule 3(1) Amendment (effective April 1, 2023) requiring an audit trail (edit log) feature in accounting software that records every alteration, timestamp, and user identity, with anti-tampering provisions.
3. **Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI)**:
   - Guidance Note on Financial Statements Preparation under Indian GAAP and Ind-AS.
   - Accounting Standard 2 (AS 2): Valuation of Inventories (FIFO and Weighted Average Costing).
